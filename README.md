# CHRONO-TERRA 🌍
### Interactive World History & Future Glacier Meltdown Planetary Simulator

An interactive dual-mode cartographic web application featuring an interactive 3D Globe, 2D Flat Map toggle, and tactile **Rotary Chrono-Dials** with **Step Granularity Switches** (10 Years, 50 Years, 100 Years, 500 Years, 1000 Years, 2500 Years).

---

## 🌟 Dual Simulation Modes

### 1. 🌊 Glacier Meltdown & Global Warming Mode (Present to Year 10,000)
- **Scientific Total Ice Meltdown Modeling**:
  - Antarctica ice sheet (~58m sea level equivalent)
  - Greenland ice sheet (~7.5m)
  - Mountain glaciers (~0.5m)
  - **Total Planetary Meltdown: +68.0 meters (~223 feet) sea level rise**.
- **Real-Time Coastal Inundation**:
  - Low-elevation flood zones fill with water and expand inland dynamically as the dial turns.
  - Florida peninsula, Netherlands, Denmark, Bangladesh, and Nile Delta completely submerged.
  - Amazon Basin ocean incursion transforms into a vast interior sea.
  - Australian Lake Eyre basin fills with marine water.
- **Shrinking Polar Ice Caps**:
  - Greenland and Antarctic ice sheets visibly melt, shrink, and vanish in 3D and 2D.
- **14+ Vulnerable Global Megacities Tracked**:
  - Miami, New York, Amsterdam, Venice, London, Alexandria, Dhaka, Shanghai, Tokyo, Jakarta, Mumbai, Bangkok, Buenos Aires, New Orleans.
  - Live status tracking: *Secure* → *Immediate Danger* → *Flooded* → *Deep Underwater*.
- **Atmospheric Greenhouse Heat Haze**:
  - Atmosphere transitions from crisp blue to a warm greenhouse haze as global temperature anomaly rises from +1.3°C to +14.5°C.

### 2. 🏛️ World History & Civilizations Mode (3000 BCE to 2026 CE)
- Spans 5,000 years of recorded human history.
- 20+ major historical empires (Sumer/Akkad, Ancient Egypt, Indus Valley, Shang/Zhou, Achaemenid Persia, Maurya, Rome, Han Dynasty, Byzantine, Abbasid Caliphate, Tang/Song, Mongols, Mali, Ottomans, Mughals, Qing, British Empire, Modern UN Order).
- Capital markers, empire territories, population estimates, and civilizational breakthroughs.
- Historic trade networks (Silk Road, Maritime Spice Route, Trans-Saharan, Spanish Galleon Route).

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Time Dial Controls

- **Step Granularity Switch**: Select from `10 Yrs`, `50 Yrs`, `100 Yrs`, `500 Yrs`, `1000 Yrs`, `2500 Yrs`.
- **Rotary Dial**: Circular drag or mouse-wheel scrolling with synthesized mechanical audio clicks.
- **Continuous Timeline Bar**: Scrub smoothly across epochs, play automated timelapse animations, or click milestone bookmarks.
- **2D / 3D Map Toggle**: Switch anytime between 3D Globe and 2D Flat Map.
